How the Engine Works

A three-stage neuro-symbolic pipeline trained on 50,000+ synthetic city topologies.

01

Graph Neural Network Encoder

Delivery coordinates, demand weights, and time windows are encoded into 64-dimensional node embeddings using a Spatial GNN with Graph Attention layers (GAT) and GraphSAGE convolutions.

02

Actor-Critic RL Policy

An Advantage Actor-Critic network makes sub-50ms routing decisions using learned road topology intuition. Invalid actions are dynamically masked to enforce capacity and time window constraints.

03

Neuro-Symbolic 2-Opt Refinement

A lightning-fast mathematical local search pass untangles any crossed paths from the neural output, combining AI intuition with mathematical proof for provably shorter routes.

The Fair Benchmark Commitment

We believe in transparent, un-handicapped comparisons. In the Dispatch Center, every routing request runs a live dual-benchmark: Waynex Neural Engine vs. the official Google OR-Tools solver.

Why Waynex Neural Route is Optimized

  • Sub-50ms Inference Policy: The GNN-RL neural network evaluates graph candidate hops instantly without recalculating mathematical MILP matrices from scratch, allowing for real-time disruption response.
  • Spatial GNN Clustering: Delivery stops are naturally clustered based on road graph topology to minimize cross-town transit time and avoid overlapping fleet territories.
  • Dynamic Capacity Action Masking: Heavy payload packages (e.g. >500kg) are dynamically masked to Heavy Diesel trucks, while lighter, clustered parcels go to EV Vans to maximize battery range.
  • Neuro-Symbolic 2-Opt Refinement: After the neural network generates its initial route, a mathematical local search pass untangles any crossed edges, guaranteeing provably shorter paths.
